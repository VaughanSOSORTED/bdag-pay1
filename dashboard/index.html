Payment links & merchant checkout

Get paid on BlockDAG with a link or QR code.

A merchant creates a payment link, the customer scans it and pays in BDAG or a stablecoin, and the blockchain holds the receipt. Zero custody: funds move payer to merchant in one transaction.

ContractFunctionNotes
PaymentLinkscreateLink(amount, token, memo)token address(0) = native BDAG, else ERC20/stablecoin
pay(id)Native checkout — must send the exact amount, single-use
payToken(id)Stablecoin checkout — needs a prior ERC20 approve
Paid eventThe onchain receipt: id, payer, payee, amount, token, memo, timestamp

tipToken(token, amount, message)Stablecoin tip with ERC20 approve
